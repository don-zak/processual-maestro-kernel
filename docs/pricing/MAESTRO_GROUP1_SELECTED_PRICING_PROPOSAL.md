## Institutional policy

- Enterprise allowances are not tied to a fixed seat count.
- 500,000 units are an enterprise entry or pilot tier.
- Core, Scale, and Strategic tiers support wider institutional adoption.
- Enterprise Custom remains contract-measured.
- Human professional services and expert review are separate unless explicitly
  included by contract.
- SLA, security, governance, deployment, and support requirements may require
  a custom commercial proposal.

## Approval boundary

This selected proposal is ready for final business review only. It does not
publish catalog prices and does not activate checkout, invoicing, settlement,
