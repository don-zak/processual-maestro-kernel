## Enterprise plan ladder

| Enterprise Scale | 3,000,000 Maestro units | Broad institutional adoption |
| Enterprise Strategic | 5,000,000+ Maestro units | Intensive strategic and highly integrated use |
| Enterprise Custom | Contract measurement | Tailored usage, SLA, support, security, and governance |

## Binding interpretation

The 500,000-unit plan is not the universal full-enterprise plan. It is the
enterprise entry tier. Institutions comparable to a national telecom operator,
large bank, ministry, or major industrial group require sizing based on actual
workload evidence.

Enterprise sizing must consider:

1. active workflows rather than employee count alone;
2. execution frequency;
3. equivalent pages and processed records;
4. integration actions;
5. verification items;
6. supervision gates;
7. normal, heavy, extreme, and custom resource bands;
8. automated jobs without direct user interaction;
9. support and SLA obligations;
10. security, governance, audit, and deployment requirements.

## Commercial principles

- Enterprise quotas are not tied to a fixed seat count.
- Additional usage may be handled by overage, tier transition, or custom contract.
- Human expert review and professional services are priced separately unless
  explicitly included by contract.
- The recommended scenario remains the default pricing basis:
  target net margin 40%, uniqueness premium 10%, BYOK provider cost excluded.
- Enterprise Pilot, Core, Scale, and Strategic prices remain proposals until approved.
- Enterprise Custom requires commercial and technical review.

## Approval boundary

This reference policy does not activate commercial runtime behavior.
No catalog publication, checkout, invoicing, settlement, entitlement
enforcement, or customer migration is authorized by this document.
